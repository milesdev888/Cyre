HOW CYRE CAUGHT IT

2024 F-150 Lariat — double pledge

14:02:11VIN tokenized by Lender A — inventory pledge recorded on-chain
14:02:38The same VIN hash appears inside a second pledge — Lender B
14:02:38Cyre: identical collateral now backing two open positions
14:02:39RISK 0.97 — FLAG raised on both positions

In the paper world, a double-floored vehicle surfaces at the next audit — weeks or months after the money moved. On-chain, both pledges are public the moment the second one lands. Cyre just has to be watching. It always is.

WHY THE ACCORD IS AMBER

2023 Accord Sport — sold, still pledged

09:14:03Retail sale event recorded for the VIN — buyer financing settled
09:14:04Cyre: pledge against the same VIN remains open
09:14:04REVIEW — normal for ~48h after sale; escalates to FLAG if the pledge isn't released

Sold-out-of-trust is the quiet one: the car leaves the lot, the payoff never happens. Cyre doesn't accuse — it starts a clock.
